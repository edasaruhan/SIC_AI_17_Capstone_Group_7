# Dataset decision — Online Retail II

Decision: SELECTED for the capstone churn experiment, 2026-09-08.
Evidence class: external dataset metadata plus project-reproduced local profile.
The local profile values and checksum below were generated by the reproducible
profiler; no business result is inferred from the repository description.

## Selection criteria and candidates

| Candidate | Temporal/customer evidence | Licence/access | Churn suitability | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UCI Online Retail II | 1,067,371 transaction lines, stable customer/invoice IDs, 2009-12-01–2011-12-09 | CC BY 4.0, DOI `10.24432/C5CG6D`, direct XLSX | Two years permit separated observation/label/test windows; cancellations and missing IDs require explicit cleaning | Selected |
| UCI Online Retail | 541,909 lines, same retailer, 2010-12-01–2011-12-09 | CC BY 4.0, DOI `10.24432/C5BW33` | Valid but roughly half the history; weaker for purchase-cycle analysis and multiple cutoffs | Rejected in favor of longer source |
| UCI Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention | 12,330 sessions; each session belongs to a different user | CC BY 4.0, DOI `10.24432/C5F88Q` | Predicts session conversion, not repeat-customer inactivity; no longitudinal customer target | Rejected |
| dunnhumby Complete Journey | 2,500 frequent-shopper households, two years, transactions and selected direct-marketing history | Official educational source; page does not state a reusable licence | Stronger promotion context, but licence/redistribution terms unresolved and frequent-shopper sampling changes population | Rejected for access/licence certainty |

## Selected source and usage

- Creator/citation: Daqing Chen (2012), *Online Retail II*, UCI Machine Learning Repository.
- Scope: a UK non-store retailer; many customers are wholesalers. This is not a
  representative sample of Turkish SMEs or consumers.
- Raw URL: `https://archive.ics.uci.edu/static/public/502/online+retail+ii.zip`.
- Repository policy: raw data is ignored and never committed. `scripts/download_dataset.py`
  retrieves only the pinned URL and rejects a checksum mismatch. Attribution and
  the CC BY 4.0 licence link remain in this decision record.
- Source columns are transaction evidence, not ready-made labels. Invoice prefixes,
  negative quantity, nonpositive price, missing IDs, duplicate rows and stock/service
  lines are profiled and handled with versioned rules. No row is silently changed.

## Fit and limitations

The source supports cutoff-safe RFM, cadence, basket, product/country and return
features. It has no advertising exposure, consent, margin, demographics, explicit
contract termination or verified intervention outcomes. Therefore the project can
evaluate future-purchase inactivity classification, not causal campaign lift or a
universally observed notion of customer churn. The untouched final period must remain
unavailable to model/tuning decisions until candidate selection is frozen.

Selection does not approve a churn window. Purchase-cycle profiling and the separate
Target Definition Memo must be completed before any label or training table is built.
