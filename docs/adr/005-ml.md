# ADR-005: Reproducible customer intelligence

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-08
Authority: Project Lead decisions conveyed in founder's full local authorization.

## Context

Churn needs credible temporal targets, evaluation and faithful explanations.

## Decision

Offline research using validated retail transactions, written dataset/target records, versioned cutoff-safe shared features, temporal holdout. Compare naive/logistic/random forest/LightGBM; SHAP, calibration, top-k and threshold analysis. MLflow tracks experiments/registry with artifact integrity. Batch and small on-demand inference use approved artifacts.

## Alternatives

Preselecting a winner, random row splits and request-time training are invalid for this design. Feast and standalone inference microservices lack operational justification.

## Consequences

Research selects dataset/windows only after evidence. Holdout stays untouched until candidate/threshold frozen. Synthetic product data never supplies academic metrics. Scores carry model/feature/target/split provenance.
