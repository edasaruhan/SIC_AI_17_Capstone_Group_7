# ADR-004: Measurement and action control

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-08
Authority: Project Lead decisions conveyed in founder's full local authorization.

## Context

Customer insight must lead to measurable, accountable actions.

## Decision

Provider-neutral Meta/Google boundaries; deterministic identity resolution and evidence-qualified last-touch baseline. Version metric/attribution/audience definitions. Persist draft, approval, queue, execution and outcome state; enforce spend ceilings, idempotency and kill switch. LLM drafts consume verified facts and cannot approve actions.

## Alternatives

Observed ROAS ranking does not establish incremental return; probabilistic identity lacks current evidence; unattended LLM spend violates requirements.

## Consequences

Provider capabilities require current official documentation and real validation before live claims. Budgets use constrained methods with uncertainty. Missing credentials leave explicit readiness gates.
